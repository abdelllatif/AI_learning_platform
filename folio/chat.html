Sure. Polymorphism means a single method can behave differently depending on the object calling it.

In your chapter 5 example, an Animal class has a makeSound() method. Both Dog and Cat override it:
  • Dog.makeSound() → "Bark"
  • Cat.makeSound() → "Meow"
Calling animal.makeSound() runs the right version automatically — that's polymorphism.
